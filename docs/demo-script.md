# PassionLedger AI - 60-Second Demo Script

This script guides you through demonstrating the core value proposition of PassionLedger AI within a 60-to-90 second timeframe.

---

## Script Flow & Visual Cues

### 1. Introduce the Hook (0:00 - 0:15)
*   **Action**: Start on the main **Dashboard Overview**. Point out the new card banner at the top: *✨ Turn Everyday Spending into Your Dream Goal*.
*   **Speech**: 
    > "Most cashbooks are reactive—explaining where your money went in the past. But **PassionLedger AI** is proactive. It connects your actual financial footprint with the dreams you care about. Let's see how."

### 2. Enter PassionLedger AI (0:15 - 0:30)
*   **Action**: Click the banner or the **Passion AI** link in the navigation menu. The visually warm landing page loads. Click **"Try the 60-Second Demo"**.
*   **Speech**: 
    > "We are immediately taken to our Passion Goals center in Demo Mode. Here, we've loaded a real-world scenario: funding a **Home Recording Studio** to produce music lessons, requiring ₹60,000 in 8 months, with ₹8,000 already saved."

### 3. Review Local Math & Feasibility (0:30 - 0:45)
*   **Action**: Hover over the progress gauge (13% saved) and show the metrics cards (monthly income ₹45k, expenses ₹40.5k, surplus ₹4.5k, required monthly savings ₹6.5k). Point to the **Challenging But Possible** status.
*   **Speech**:
    > "PassionLedger calculates the metrics locally and securely: our required monthly savings rate is ₹6,500, but our safe monthly capacity is only ₹3,600—creating a monthly gap. The local calculations indicate this status is *Challenging but Possible*."

### 4. Run the What-If Simulator (0:45 - 1:05)
*   **Action**: Slide the **"Reduce Optional Monthly Spending"** slider to **₹2,000**. Watch the Estimated Completion Date, Funding Gap, and Recharts projection curve update instantly on the screen!
*   **Speech**:
    > "Using the What-If Simulator, let's adjust discretionary costs. By planning a ₹2,000 reduction in monthly food delivery and subscriptions, our simulated savings rate climbs. The projection runway updates immediately, showing we can hit our goal 2 months early!"

### 5. Generate AI Passion Plan & Export (1:05 - 1:20)
*   **Action**: Click **"Generate My AI Passion Plan"**. See the rotating loading message and the final output showing the personalized plan, first-week checklist, and milestone checkpoints. Click **"Download Passion Plan PDF"**.
*   **Speech**:
    > "By requesting an AI plan, only our aggregates are sent to the Gemini API—never raw personal transactions. Gemini delivers structured planning advice: optimizations, first-week checklists, and milestones. We can download this complete plan as a beautifully formatted PDF report instantly."

### 6. Summary (1:20 - 1:30)
*   **Speech**:
    > "PassionLedger AI: turning daily tracking into meaningful action. Complete privacy, offline availability, and realistic plans for the dreams you care about. Thank you!"
